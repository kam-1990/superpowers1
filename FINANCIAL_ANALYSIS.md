# Sakora Design Inc. - January 2026 Financial Performance Analysis

**Report Date:** February 7, 2026
**Period Analyzed:** January 2025 through January 2026
**Source:** Profit and Loss by Month (Accrual Basis)

---

## Executive Summary

January 2026 was **the second-best month in the 13-month period**, delivering a net profit of **$36,021** — a dramatic turnaround from the prior three consecutive loss months (Oct-Dec 2025). This was driven by strong revenue ($102,166) combined with historically low cost of goods sold ($12,899), yielding an exceptional **87.4% gross margin**.

## Revenue Analysis

- **Services revenue** of $101,318 was the **3rd highest** on record, behind only March 2025 ($110,187) and June 2025 ($104,193).
- Revenue more than **doubled** from December 2025 ($45,692), which had been part of a Q4 decline.
- **Billable Expense Income** ($808) was the highest in any single month.
- The Q4 2025 revenue slump (Oct: $53.5K, Nov: $50.3K, Dec: $45.7K) has been decisively reversed.

## Cost of Goods Sold Analysis

- COGS of $12,899 was **by far the lowest** in the 13-month period (prior low: $30,553 in Mar 2025).
- **Technical Wages** dropped to $11,346 (vs. 12-mo avg of $31,490) — a 64% reduction.
- **No subcontractor costs** were incurred in January 2026.
- **No application fees** were charged.
- **New line item:** "COS - Wages" appeared for the first time at $2,596, suggesting a payroll restructuring.

## Expense Analysis

- Total expenses of $53,246 were **elevated** (+41% vs. average), driven by:
  - **Payroll Expenses** of $34,252 — the **highest** month on record (vs. avg $20,215). Wages jumped to $28,942 (nearly doubled from the typical $15,000).
  - **Subscriptions** at $2,244 — above average ($1,220).
  - **Advertising** at $4,302 + $1,125 promotional = $5,427 total — above average.
- Partially offset by:
  - **Rent & Utilities** down to $2,700 (vs. avg $3,486) — no maintenance or utility charges.
  - **Meals & Entertainment** at just $499 (vs. avg $1,576).
  - **No repair & maintenance** outside of $255 routine charge.
  - **Legal fees** at $551 — below the $1,015 average.

## Profitability

- **Net profit of $36,021** ranks as the **2nd most profitable month**, behind only March 2025 ($46,412).
- **Net margin of 35.3%** is the **3rd best** (after March at 42.1%).
- This breaks a streak of **3 consecutive loss months** (Oct: -$30.4K, Nov: -$14.7K, Dec: -$24.0K).
- Over the 13-month window, only 4 months were profitable: Mar 2025, Jun 2025, Sep 2025, and Jan 2026.

## Key Observations & Risks

1. **Revenue cyclicality**: The business shows a pronounced boom/bust pattern. High-revenue months (Mar, Jun, Sep, Jan) alternate with lean months. This suggests project-based or seasonal revenue.

2. **COGS restructuring**: The dramatic drop in Technical Wages and appearance of a new "COS - Wages" line suggests a staffing change (possibly moving from contractors to employees, or reducing headcount). This could indicate either a strategic restructuring or a temporary gap.

3. **Payroll shift**: Fixed payroll expenses nearly doubled in January 2026 ($34,252 vs. $20,215 avg). Combined with the COGS wage reduction, this may reflect reclassification of technical staff from COGS to operating expenses.

4. **Only 4 of 13 months profitable**: The company has been unprofitable in 9 of the last 13 months, with cumulative losses over the period. January 2026 is a strong rebound but sustainability is uncertain given the historical pattern.

5. **Full-year 2025 net loss**: Calendar year 2025 (Jan-Dec) produced a cumulative net loss of approximately **-$82,835**.
